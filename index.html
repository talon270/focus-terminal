drills

pick one. two minutes each.

you will get better at each drill. whether that carries over to your work is unproven — studies of brain training find the gains mostly stay with the task practised. use them as a warm-up. each leans on something different: reaction on alertness, schulte on visual search, 2-back on holding things in mind while new things arrive, digit span on sequence memory.

breathe

reset between drills

slow, evenly paced breathing with a longer exhale settles the body down fast. follow the bar; it fills on the in-breath and drains on the out-breath.
